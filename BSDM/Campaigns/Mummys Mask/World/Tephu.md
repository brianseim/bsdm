[[The Inn of the Desert Winds]]

**Local Specialty: Tephu Papyrus**

While standard papyrus costs 4 sp, you can purchase unique varieties in Tephu's markets:

- **Impossible Papyrus:** Extremely durable and hard to tear (3 gp/sheet).
- **Set’s Papyrus:** Luminescent writing that glows in the dark (5 gp/sheet).
- **Sphinx Papyrus:** Robust but thin enough to fold to the size of a coin (10 gp/sheet).
- **Tephu Spellbooks:** Considered some of the finest in the Inner Sea, these are often created as ornate scrolls rather than bound books (Cost 50% more than normal spellbooks).
